Overview

Dr. Fawad Nasir is a compassionate, evidence-based physician who cares for adults with complex medical conditions.

He has worked across internal medicine, pediatrics, general surgery, and orthopedics at a tertiary care teaching hospital, and is now completing four years of postgraduate training in Internal Medicine through the FCPS program. Those who work with him see a physician who collaborates closely with nurses, specialists, and allied health staff, and who explains diagnoses and plans to families in a clear, supportive way.

He is ECFMG certified, has passed USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, and is seeking a residency position in the United States.

What Dr. Nasir offers

Care is provided under attending supervision as part of his training. Services reflect the work described in his CV.

Adult internal medicine consultation

Detailed history, physical examination, differential diagnosis, and a clear management plan for adults with single or multi-system illness.

Chronic disease follow-up

Ongoing review of cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, endocrine, and infectious conditions, with plans that fit each patient.

Acute illness assessment

Prompt evaluation of new or worsening problems, with diagnostic tests ordered and reviewed as clinically indicated.

Diagnostic coordination

Workups arranged with laboratory and radiology services, with results explained in plain language.

Bedside procedures and emergencies

Lumbar puncture and other bedside procedures, plus BLS and ALS trained emergency stabilization.
